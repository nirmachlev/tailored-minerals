05 / Why Now

Four vectors,
one convergence.

VECTOR 01

Geopolitical pressure.

Trade weaponization made rare-earth dependency a national-security item. Reshoring is funded.

VECTOR 02

ESG pressure.

Compliance regimes punish dirty extraction and reward designed substitutes. Procurement demands it.

VECTOR 03

Generative > predictive.

The leap from "predict properties" to "design molecules" makes inverse design tractable.

VECTOR 04

Robotic labs ⇒ commodity.

Self-driving labs (SDLs) shifted from custom builds to procurable units. Hardware barrier collapsed.
